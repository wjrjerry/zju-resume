\textit{Li Lab} \titlebreak
\textbf{Project Overview:} A repository-level framework for evaluating and improving coding-agent security. \textbf{My Contribution:}
\begin{itemize}[nosep, topsep=\intraProjectListTopSep]

\end{itemize}

\projectsep

\textbf{SeekJudge: Reward Modeling for Computer-Use Agents} \quad \href{https://github.com/ZJUSCL/SeekJudge}{[GitHub]} \hfill Mar. 2026 -- May 2026 \titlebreak
\textit{ZJU Scaling Logger (ZJUSCL)} \titlebreak
\textbf{Project Overview:} A practical model-based reward framework that replaces brittle rule-based supervision for computer-use agents. It uses four role-specialized agents---Condense, Ground, Seek, and Analyze---to verify long-horizon GUI trajectories through an iterative Seek--Analyze loop, and distills their capabilities into a specialized 9B judge model that matches or surpasses rule-based rewards in online reinforcement learning while providing step-level judgments at lower cost.

\textbf{My Contributions:}
\begin{itemize}[nosep, topsep=\intraProjectListTopSep]
    \item Developed the Condense--Ground--Seek--Analyze judging pipeline, using a Seek--Analyze loop to gather evidence from long screenshot and action sequences and determine task completion.
    \item Built the trajectory-processing and HTTP reward-serving pipeline, supporting offline batch evaluation and online reinforcement learning.
\end{itemize}
