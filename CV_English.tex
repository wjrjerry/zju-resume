\section{\makebox[\widthof{\faBullseye}][c]{\color{CVBlue}\faBullseye}\ Purpose of Visit}

In particular, I hope to deepen my understanding of how large language models learn from feedback, how different training objectives shape reasoning behavior, and how these methods can be evaluated reliably in practical interactive settings.

This internship is directly connected to my undergraduate training at Zhejiang University and to my current research experience in artificial intelligence. I will use the internship to learn research methodology in an active academic environment, strengthen my ability to implement and evaluate state-of-the-art methods, and develop an independent research project that can support my future study in computer science.

\section{\makebox[\widthof{\faFlask}][c]{\color{CVBlue}\faFlask}\ Proposed Research Direction}
The proposed research topic is \textbf{learning from feedback for multi-modal and agentic large language models}. The project will study how language models and agent systems can use feedback signals to improve reasoning, decision-making, and task completion. The work may involve literature review, implementation of recent methods, validation experiments, analysis of model behavior, and development of new techniques for more reliable learning and evaluation.

The main research questions include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item How do different feedback signals, such as human preference, model-generated judgments, task rewards, or trajectory-level evaluations, affect the reasoning behavior of large language models?
    \item How can multi-modal information, including screenshots, visual states, structured records, or other non-text inputs, be incorporated into training and evaluation pipelines?
    \item How can agent-based systems be evaluated beyond final-answer matching, especially when task success depends on intermediate actions and environmental state changes?
    \item What practical training objectives can improve model reliability while keeping the evaluation process reproducible and scientifically meaningful?
\end{itemize}

This phase will focus on correctness, reproducibility, and understanding the strengths and limitations of existing methods.

\textbf{Weeks 5--7: Experimentation and method development.} I will conduct validation experiments, compare training objectives and feedback designs, analyze model behavior, and propose possible improvements. Depending on project progress, this may include reward-modeling methods, judge-based evaluation, trajectory-level supervision, or multi-modal feedback integration.

\textbf{Weeks 8--9: Analysis, writing, and presentation.} I will summarize results, prepare figures and tables, write a research report or paper draft, and present the work to the supervisor and research group. If the results are sufficiently strong, I will continue improving the project after the internship toward submission to an appropriate academic venue.
